## Development Milestones & Cadence
* **Core Mechanism:** Maintaining the 4-second rolling frame buffer in p5.js via browser RAM.
* **Physical Setup:** Dark room environment, circle layout, and external handheld light sources (torches/lamps) aimed by audience members at the looker's eyes.
* **Build Cadence:** Short, focused evening build sessions, rapid physical prototyping with cardboard and cutters, and iterative browser testing.
